Free UK-style cookie policy generator. Answer 3 short steps about the cookies your site actually uses and copy or download your draft on screen.

== Description ==

Answer three short steps — about the business, which of five cookie types the site uses (plus any named third-party services and whether a consent banner is shown) — and get a structured, UK-style cookie policy draft with your own details filled in, describing only the cookie types you ticked.

Use the shortcode `[altus_cookie_policy_generator]` on any page.

This open-source version contains NO lead capture, NO email sending, and NO data storage. The policy is generated entirely in the visitor's browser and shown on screen; copy it or download it as .txt or .html.

IMPORTANT: the output is a draft template only, NOT legal or financial advice. Have it reviewed by a professional (for example a solicitor) before publishing it or relying on it.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin zip via Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, or extract it into `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate the plugin.
3. Add `[altus_cookie_policy_generator]` to any page and publish it.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is it really free? =

Yes. Generating, copying and downloading the policy are all free, with no account and no email address required.

= Is this legal advice? =

No. It is a draft template based on your answers, structured around UK cookie rules. Have a professional review it before publishing, and check that every section matches what your site actually does.

= Are my answers sent or stored anywhere? =

No. Generation happens in the visitor's browser. There is no server endpoint, no database table, no email and no file write in this version.

= What if my site uses no banner but sets non-essential cookies? =

The draft says so in plain English — it includes a warning that consent should come first for non-essential cookies. Fix the banner situation on your site, then regenerate.
